About

Who's Behind
the Light

I'm Alex, the motion designer behind Green Light Studios. Most designers think in stories. I also think in logical systems. That combination lives in every frame I build.

Based in Northern Colorado, working with businesses and studios across the USA and around the world. I build motion graphics for brands that need to look like they belong.

Before motion graphics I spent years in technical consulting, building a brain for systems and logic that art school never teaches. I brought all of that back with me.

The work I want to make is clean but surprisingly magical. Technically precise but emotionally felt. The kind of motion that makes a viewer pause mid scroll and rewind.
